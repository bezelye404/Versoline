An RSS reader and podcast player for Mac

Versoline reads RSS and Atom feeds, plays podcasts and follows YouTube channels and Reddit communities. It is written in Swift and SwiftUI, uses only Apple's frameworks, and keeps everything on your Mac.

Download for macOS View source on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 15 Sequoia or later · Apple silicon and Intel

What it does

Feeds, articles, podcasts and video in one window, with the reading view at the centre.

Feeds and sources

RSS 2.0 and Atom, YouTube channels and playlists, Reddit subreddits and users. Paste a site address and Versoline looks for the feed. OPML import and export with folders.

A reading view without a web view

Articles are laid out with native SwiftUI text: headings, lists, quotes, code, tables and images with captions. Menus, share bars and ads are removed, and the original page is one click away.

Same story, once

When several feeds report the same news, the list shows one row with the number of sources. A Top Stories list shows what three or more of your feeds cover. Grouping is computed on your Mac and can be turned off.

Typography and themes

System, serif, sans-serif and monospaced fonts, font size and line height, ten colour palettes and Bionic Reading.

Translate on your Mac

Articles in another language are translated with Apple's Translation framework, so the text does not leave your Mac.

Highlights, notes and search

Find in Article, highlights and notes on paragraphs, and a saved reading position in long articles. Bookmarks can be indexed in Spotlight if you turn that on.

Podcasts and video

Search the Apple Podcasts directory, stream or download episodes, set a sleep timer and a queue, and play from 0.75x to 2x. Videos play in an embedded player or in your browser.

Rules and muted keywords

Mark new articles as read, bookmark them, or hide topics you do not want to see. Feed Health lists feeds that fail or have gone quiet.

Backup and nearby sync

Back up the whole library as one file. Optionally sync your own Macs directly over the local network after pairing them with a code. No server is involved.

Privacy and data

Versoline has no account system, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything it stores lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Versoline. The network requests it makes go directly from your Mac, and each has a reason.

RequestTo whomWhen
Feed updatesThe sites whose feeds you followOn refresh
Article pagesThe site of an articleWhen you open it
Site iconsThe site itselfWhen a feed is shown
Article images, YouTube thumbnailsTheir publishersWhen shown
Podcast searchApple's iTunes Search APIWhen you search
Curated feed catalogThe project's GitHub repositoryWhen Add Feed is opened
Translation language packsApple, through macOSFirst use of a language pair

Story grouping, translation, Spotlight indexing, highlights and notes all run on your Mac. The app has no third-party dependencies, and the source is public so you can check any of this.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg from the latest release.
  2. Open it and drag Versoline to Applications.

Versoline is signed ad hoc, without a paid Apple Developer certificate, so macOS may say the developer cannot be verified on first launch. Right-click the app and choose Open, or run:

xattr -cr /Applications/Versoline.app

If you used the earlier name easyRSS, Versoline imports its data on first launch and leaves the old folder untouched.

Build from source

You need macOS 15 or later, Xcode 16 or later and XcodeGen.

git clone https://github.com/bezelye404/Versoline.git
cd Versoline
xcodegen generate
xcodebuild -project Versoline.xcodeproj \
  -scheme Versoline -configuration Release build

Frequently asked questions

Does Versoline need an account or an internet connection?

No account. It needs the network to fetch feeds and articles. Articles and episodes you have already loaded or downloaded stay available offline.

Where is my data, and how do I move it to another Mac?

In ~/Library/Application Support/Versoline. Use File > Export Backup and Restore Backup, export your subscriptions as OPML, or turn on nearby sync between your own Macs.

How do I import my subscriptions from another reader?

Export an OPML file from it and choose File > Import OPML, or open the file with Versoline.

Can I follow a site that has no visible RSS link?

Paste the site address in Add Feed. If the site advertises a feed, or uses a common feed path, Versoline finds it.

Which languages does the app support?

English and Turkish.

Is it free?

Yes. It is released under the MIT license, and the source code is on GitHub.
