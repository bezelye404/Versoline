# Versoline 0.4.1

A small interface fix.

## Fixed

- The counts in the sidebar section headers (Smart Streams, Feeds, folders, Pinned) sat further to the right than the counts in the rows below them. They now line up.

## Notes

- Requires macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
- The app is signed ad hoc. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, right-click the app and choose **Open**, or run `xattr -cr /Applications/Versoline.app`.
- Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).
